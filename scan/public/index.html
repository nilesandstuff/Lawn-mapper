Checking what this phone offers…

Before you go out: references and check points on the photo.

References are spots you can aim at where they meet the ground: driveway, sidewalk and patio corners first (the lidar and the photo see the ground there), then a fence post or a shed corner, a house corner last (its roof may overhang the wall). Two line the map up; more is better, spread round the lawn. Check points are other corners you will aim at to measure how close the fix is. Tap the photo to add one; drag a point to move it; pinch or + to zoom.

    Reading the lidar…

    The test, on each phone
    1. Start AR. Point the camera at the lawn and move the phone slowly until the crosshair turns green on the ground.
    2. Stand within 10 ft of the first reference, aim the crosshair at it where it meets the ground, tap Mark; then the second. From then on the top line says where you are. As you walk, look round at the house, fences, bushes and trunks: the lidar match takes over when it can ("You are here (lidar fix…)"). Mark each check point the same way: it says how far off the map position is.
    3. Stand at a spot you can find again (a sprinkler head, a paving corner). Aim at it from about a metre and tap Drop pin. The dot is green when the ground comes from the phone's depth picture, amber when only from a plane (planes reach past where the phone saw them: that put pins under the back yard on the first walk).
    4. Walk about 20 m away and back. Aim at the same spot again and tap Check pin: it says how far the pin has moved from the spot, as placed and (with a *) as its anchor carries it. Drop a pin in the front and one in the back, then do one deliberate swing of the phone mid-walk, and check both afterwards: the first walk lost 3 m there.
    5. Two marks a tape says are 10 m (or 30 ft) apart: aim at one, tap Measure, aim at the other, tap Measure. Afterwards type what the tape said.
    6. Tap Exit. The results save to the site by themselves (distances and counts only, no positions); the chat reads them from there. Type the tape's number beside a measure when you have it.

    Passing: the pin within a foot of its spot after the walk, and the measure within 2% of the tape, on each engine. Turn slowly; a turn faster than a quarter turn a second blurs the picture.

    Walk twins : each walk's points, track and pictures over the photo and the lidar.

    Walk inspector : every depth point's label, confidence and match verdict, the best-fit credits, the placements against the check points, the pictures with their points, in 3D.
