How to use this

Look: one finger turns, two fingers move and zoom; every point is drawn whole. The focus band below belongs to Inspect, Erase and Bring back. Tap a point to make it the centre: what is in focus is a band at the centre's distance from you, as deep as the "In focus" slider says. Everything nearer or farther is faint and cannot be erased or inspected, so nothing in front of or behind what you are working on is touched by accident.

Inspect: tap a point to see the photo taken when it was seen, with the point marked on it. Erase: drag over points in focus (the red ring is the brush). Bring back: turn on "show erased" and drag over red points. Two fingers still move the view in every mode. Save keeps what you erased with the walk; it is also saved by itself a few seconds after each stroke.

Nothing is deleted from the walk: erased points are a list kept beside it, which the tools then leave out.
