How this works

Each photo is shown with the shapes it was cut into. Tap a shape and give it a name: your own words, the categories that matter to you. Names you have used become buttons.

A name goes to the whole thing: the same wall, shed or tree as the tools matched it across photos. So naming it once names it everywhere, and it shows on the other photos as you move through them.

Where a name has landed on something it should not (the tools matched two different things as one), tap that shape, choose "Only this shape, in this photo", and give it its right name. Every such correction tells the tools exactly where their matching went wrong and from how far.

A white outline is named; a yellow one is not yet; a grey one has no depth under it (the phone measured no distance there), so it cannot be placed in 3D and need not be named. Saved by itself a few seconds after each change.
