Soil moisture readings

Spot readings with a hand meter, to check the water model against the ground.

This visit

    Aim at the grass, not bare soil, from about a foot away, your shadow off the spot. Best on a sunny afternoon (1-3 pm) or before dawn on a clear night.

    Meter

    Kept on this phone for the next visit.

    Brown dots are tree trunks you marked (the model checks its own trunk finder against them, and uses them for the trees' roots). The green line is the lawn as traced on the saved map, on the photo it was traced on: the model judges a reading by that line, so put the dot where you stood against it. Grey rings are spots read on earlier visits here: tap one to read the same spot again. Readings are kept in the water tool's own database with the county parcel number, never the street address.
