What this can and can't see

  • The ground as the laser saw it on the survey date shown. Anything regraded, built or planted since is not in it.
  • Hollows about 25 ft across or wider that hold about 2 inches or more are usually found. Ones under about 10 ft across are not: the survey is too coarse.
  • Until you add your downspouts, it guesses where each roof lets its water off.
  • The soil is the USDA soil survey's: its layers, how much water they hold and how fast water soaks in, as the survey measured them. A soak test (coming) will check it on your lawn.
  • The weather is NASA's daily record and the National Weather Service forecast, on a grid about 30 miles across: a local storm it missed is not in the plan.
  • Storm drains and yard drains look like ponds to it, because it can't see the pipe.

An early test. Lidar: USGS 3DEP. Soil: USDA NRCS Soil Survey. Weather: NASA POWER and the National Weather Service.
