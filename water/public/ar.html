1. Before you go outside: points you can find on the ground and on this photo.

Choose references all round the lawn, the back as well as the front, so there is always one within a short walk (corners of a patio, driveway, sidewalk or shed where they meet the ground, a head already mapped; a house's roof edge leans on the photo, so its corner on the ground may not be where the roof shows it) and, to measure accuracy, a few check points (other corners or heads). Tap the photo to add one; tap near a mapped head to use it; drag a point to move it.

Pinch or use + to zoom; drag the photo to move round it once zoomed.

    2. Outside, in AR.

    Stand close to a reference (within about 10 ft), aim the crosshair at it where it meets the ground (it turns green on the ground), and tap Mark. After two references the map is lined up: aim at each sprinkler head from close by and tap Place head. The phone drifts as you walk and turn, so whenever you pass a reference, mark it again (tap it on the small map, then Mark): the page trusts the marks made the shortest walk away. Turn slowly. Mark the check points too: the page says how far off each lands.
