1. Before you go outside: points you can find on the ground and on this photo.

Choose references (at least two, 30 ft or more apart: house or patio corners at ground level, a driveway corner, a head already mapped) and, to measure accuracy, a few check points (other corners or heads). Tap the photo to add one; tap near a mapped head to use it; drag a point to move it.

    2. Outside, in AR.

    Stand back from a reference, aim the crosshair at it where it meets the ground (it turns green on the ground), and tap Mark. After two references the map is lined up: aim at each sprinkler head and tap Place head. Mark the check points too, any time: the page says how far off each lands. Move slowly; if the camera is covered or swung fast, the AR loses its place: mark two references again.
