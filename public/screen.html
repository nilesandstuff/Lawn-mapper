Is there a grass lawn here, of any size, that is mostly visible?

Yes if you can see grass and could draw its edge: a front lawn, a back garden, a verge. Size does not matter and neither does condition — dormant, patchy and shaded all count.

No if there is no grass, if the picture is too old, blurred or cloudy to trace from, if it is a farm field, a park, a car park or a block of flats, or if a tree covers so much that the lawn is guesswork. A stranger being paid for five minutes cannot resolve what you cannot.
