Keep it if you would rather tidy this than draw it again. That is the bar — not whether it is right. The edges get corrected anyway.

This page answers one question only: did this person do adequate work. Keeping a map credits them and sends it to the ordinary review queue — it does not put it in the corpus. Tidying it, the canopy grade and the inferred check all still happen there.

Not good enough, hard lawn when the outline is not worth keeping but you can see why: dense canopy, a boundary that genuinely is not readable, imagery that turned out worse than it looked when screened. This counts as a pass for them, and the map is kept as a candidate — it is a traced outline on a hard property, and you may want to finish it yourself.

Bad map when the outline is not an attempt at this lawn — the automatic shape waved through, a rectangle over the whole plot, grass and drive and roof all together. This is the only one that counts against somebody, the only one that throws the map away, and four of these in five stops them. The property itself goes back in the queue for somebody else.
