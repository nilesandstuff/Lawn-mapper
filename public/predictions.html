Tap any picture to open it full size — pinch or scroll to zoom, drag to pan, double-tap to fit. The list shows about a quarter of the detail each way, and the things worth arguing about are finer than that.

Green with no outline over it is lawn it gave up on; outline over bare ground is lawn it invented. The handle count is the one to watch: twenty round a lawn is a shape you can nudge, two hundred is a shape you would delete and redraw.

Every number under a picture describes the outline, not the model's raw answer — tracing fills holes under about 60 sq ft and smooths every edge, and each row says how much that was worth. The % out pill is the exception: that is the run's own figure, from the mask, so it still matches the training table.
