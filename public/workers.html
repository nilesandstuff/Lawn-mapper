Waiting is maps you have not graded. A new crowd worker is held after five of them and cannot do more until you look, so a number here is somebody stopped rather than somebody idle.

Passing counts kept and excused over everything reviewed. Below 80% at a gate is what ends a crowd worker's run; trusted workers and volunteers are not gated at all.

Typical is the median time per map, which is the number a paid platform judges your reward against.

Owed counts approved maps only — a lawn marked as a hard one is not held against anybody and does not pay. Green means it is over the minimum payout and can actually be sent; grey means it is not there yet. A red note under a name means they have not said where to send it.
