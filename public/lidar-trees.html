Lidar trees: where the laser found something over 2 m tall that splits its pulses (or the survey called vegetation), and not a roof. From the lidar alone, no photo. Dashed: not on the lawn.

Dense: the part of those trees that few pulses get through. In a leaf-off flight, an evergreen or foliage too dense for grass. The flight's dates are in the line above the picture.

Tree model: the canopy the photo model found (workflow 28), only where it touches the lawn.

Lawn as traced, and inferred: the lawn you marked as under a tree.

The lidar is usually years older than the photo, so a tree cut down or planted since will disagree. Tap a colour below to hide or show it. One finger pans, two zoom.
