    How to correct the shape
    • In Extend to road, tap a line to slide that whole edge out, or tap a white corner dot to drag, add or delete that one point.
    • Tapping the line wins over tapping a corner, so extending stays the easy gesture — aim right at a dot when you want the point itself.
    • Delete selected removes a shape you don't mow.
    • Draw it myself adds another patch — areas add up.
    • Extend to road pushes an edge out past the property line without twisting it, for the strip between your line and the kerb.
    • Yellow dots are corners taken straight from the county map.
    • The AI finds grass inside your property line only — the neighbours' lawn is trimmed off automatically.

    Measurements come from satellite imagery and public county records. Treat them as a close estimate, not a survey.
