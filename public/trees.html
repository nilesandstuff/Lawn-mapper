Run workflow 28. Tree maps for labelling. It finds the canopy on every approved map and leaves the part over each lawn here to label.

Console

The yellow outlines are the tree model's canopy, only where it touches the lawn (the green line). Say what each part of it is.

Brush paints the chosen kind, only inside the yellow outlines. Fill paints a whole outline with one tap. Erase (a kind) takes a label off. Unlabelled canopy shows as a pale wash, so you can see what is left.

Evergreen: needles or leaves all year. In leaf: a broadleaf tree with its leaves. Bare: a broadleaf tree without them, the ground showing through. Not a tree: the model was wrong (a shadow, a hedge, a roof).

Points moves a corner of an outline (drag it); Delete point removes the one you last touched. Move pans with one finger. Two fingers always pan and zoom.

Save keeps it as a draft; Done saves and goes to the next map. Leaving a map with changes saves them.
