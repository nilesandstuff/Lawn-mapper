Workflow 8, "find county imagery", looks for them map by map.

Console

Two photos of the same frame, stacked: the Mapbox photo the outlines were traced on, and the county photo over it. Both are the banked files themselves, exactly what training is given. The sliders fade each one; Flip swaps between them at full strength, which is the quickest way to see a shift.

Green is the traced lawn, orange dashed the parts marked inferred, yellow dashed the property line -- drawn the way training rasterises them. On a good map, roof edges, kerbs and fences in the county photo sit on the same ones in Mapbox, and the green outline follows the lawn edge in both.

If the county photo is off, the arrows move it 10 cm a press (the outlines stay put: they belong to Mapbox). Then Lines up saves the verdict and the nudge; the photo is cut again with the nudge on the next re-bank pass. Don't use keeps this map on Mapbox in a county-imagery training run.
