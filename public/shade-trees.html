The trees as the shade model sees them

Tap a spot for its numbers: what the model took it for, the heights it stops light between, and how much gets through.

Each 1 m square is drawn from the lowest to the highest height where the model has something that stops light, as the shade map uses it. Tilt and turn the map with two fingers to look under the crowns. The trees stand where the laser measured them; on the photo, tall things lean by a meter or two. "Lidar points" shows the laser's own returns to compare.
