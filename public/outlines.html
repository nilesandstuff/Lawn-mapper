Run workflow 25. It photographs places with a public outline of something that is not lawn and leaves them here as drafts.

Console

The whole photo is on screen, fitted between the bars; it is exactly what the detector is shown, and anything past its edge is cut off there.

Tap an outline to drop it (grey, dashed); tap again to keep it. Drag an outline to move it on its own. The arrows move all of them, 25 cm a press. Each new picture opens with the outlines already lined up with the photo where that was clear; Line up does it again, As delivered puts them back where the public map has them.

Pinch, or the zoom button, to zoom; drag empty photo to look around. Nothing here scrolls the page.

Approve when everything outlined is certainly not lawn; Reject the whole example otherwise. Only what is kept on screen when you approve is ever taught, and going back over an approved one and approving again replaces that record.
