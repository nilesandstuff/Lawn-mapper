Route Planner

Route Reader

For Android phones. Open your job list, tap one green button, and the route planner opens with all the stops. No long screenshots.

Download Route Reader

Android 8 or newer.

Set it up (once)

Do this the first time only. It takes about 3 minutes. The exact words differ a little between phones.

  1. Download it. Tap Download Route Reader above.

    If Chrome says the file might be harmful, tap Download anyway. It says that about every app that doesn't come from the Play Store.

  2. Install it. Tap Open on the download message (or find route-reader.apk in your Downloads and tap it), then Install.

    If it says Chrome isn't allowed to install apps: tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, go back, and tap Install.

    If Play Protect warns you: tap More details, then Install anyway. (If it offers to scan the app, you can say no.)

  3. Open Route Reader and tap 1. Turn on Route Reader.

    In the list that opens, find Route Reader (it may be inside Installed apps or Downloaded apps), tap it, switch it on, and tap Allow.

  4. If the switch is greyed out, or it says Restricted setting: Android blocks this for apps not from the Play Store until you allow it.

    In Route Reader, tap If it is greyed out: allow restricted settings. On the App info screen, tap the three dots at the top right, then Allow restricted settings, and enter your PIN if asked. Then do step 3 again.

    No three dots? Try step 3 once first (so Android shows the warning), then come back here.

  5. Done. Route Reader now says it is ON, and a green Read route button floats over your apps.

Every day

  1. Open the app with today's jobs, on the list of jobs.

  2. Tap the green Read route button.

  3. Wait, and don't touch the screen. It scrolls to the top, then down to the bottom, reading as it goes.

  4. The route planner opens with the stops. Check them, then tap Plan fastest route.

The button is in the way? Drag it up or down.

If something's wrong

No green button: Route Reader has been switched off. Open it and do step 3 again. Some phones switch helper apps off to save battery: Settings, Apps, Route Reader, Battery, Unrestricted.

Stops missing or wrong: fix them in the planner. If no stops come out at all, the planner shows a code. Send that code to whoever looks after this.

A new version: download and install it the same way. It installs over the old one; you may need to switch it on again (step 3).

Route Reader does nothing until you tap its button. It reads only the screen you are on, and sends what it read to the route planner, which keeps it for a day.
